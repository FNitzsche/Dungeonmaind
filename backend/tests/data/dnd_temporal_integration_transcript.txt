The party left Waterdeep at dawn and followed the road north of the city toward Blackwood Forest. Three hours later, they reached an old bridge beside the river. Aric checked the ruined cart and found 25 gold pieces, 14 arrows, and a broken shield marked with the number 7.

“We should keep moving,” Mira said. “Tomorrow morning we need to reach Castle Ravenwatch.”

The group continued through the forest. In two hours, the trees became dense and the road disappeared beneath the undergrowth. They discovered a cave east of the river and decided to rest inside.

After a short rest, Kael examined the entrance. He rolled 18 on his investigation check and noticed three sets of footprints leading deeper into the cave. The wizard still had 2 spell slots remaining, and the fighter had 31 hit points.

Two rounds later, a group of goblins attacked from behind a stone wall. During the fight, one goblin dealt 12 damage to Mira. Kael’s spell remained active for 3 rounds. At the end of your next turn, the magical barrier will disappear.

After a long rest, the party continued toward the mountain. Later that evening, they reached a village near the castle.

The innkeeper warned them, “The cult performs its ritual every other night. The next ceremony begins at 8 PM.”

Mira looked at the map. “Then we have to enter the castle before midnight.”

The innkeeper continued, “The eastern gate opens once per day. On Friday, the guards change shifts at 10:30 am.”

The party agreed to leave the village the next morning.

On the 12th of Eleasis, they finally entered Castle Ravenwatch. The entrance stood behind the tower and across from an abandoned temple.

Inside the dungeon, Aric found door 12, a chest containing 50 silver pieces, and a corridor approximately 30 feet long. None of those numbers indicated time.

For the next two hours, the party searched the dungeon. Half an hour later, they discovered the ritual chamber.

“The prisoners will be sacrificed within three days,” said the captured priest. “You must stop the cult before the next night.”

That evening, the party established a camp outside the castle. They planned to attack at sunset.

The following morning, if they survived, they would travel back to Waterdeep.
